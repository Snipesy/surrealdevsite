## Note on VINs

Surreal Development is involved in Automotive Vehicles which are considered identifying information by the European government. As explained in the Privacy Policy, the VIN data is the responsibility of the end user to manage and respond to requests (such as GDPR). Deleting vehicles can be done with the Gretio app.

In exceptional circumstances, Surreal Development can manually remove VIN data. However, you must prove your ownership of the vehicle. Without this information we cannot process the request. This information will be deleted after processing the request. Surreal Development will only consider VINs as personal information while the vehicle is under your possession.

Documents that can prove ownership:

- Vehicle registration showing the date you obtained the vehicle.
- Vehicle registration showing the current date, or a bill of sale.
- Any other records (DMV, tax records, or equivalent).
