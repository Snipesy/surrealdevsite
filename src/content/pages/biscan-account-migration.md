Follow these steps to migrate a BiScan purchase to a Gretio account.

- Purchases before January 1st, 2022 will receive a lifetime Gretio All Access Subscription.
- Purchases between January 1st, 2022 and January 1st, 2023 will receive a 1 year Gretio All Access Subscription.

## 1. Download Gretio and sign in via Apple or Google

Use the link below to download Gretio.

[Gretio Downloads](/gretio/)

Once downloaded, sign in.

- **Android:** Navigate to the more menu, scroll down and tap "Sign In". Use either a Google or Apple account.
- **iOS:** The sign in is presented when you open the application.

After you have signed in, you may close the application. If you skip this step, we will be unable to find your account.

## 2. Fill in your information

**Important:** If you Sign in with Apple and use the "Hide My Email" feature you must supply your private email. You can find this in your iCloud settings. [Click here for more information.](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/create-and-manage-hide-my-email-addresses-iphcb02e76f7/ios)

## 3. Find your BiScan Transaction ID

[Log in to Google Payments](https://pay.google.com/), find the BiScan purchase you made and tap on it. Find the "Transaction ID" field. The ID will start with "GPA".

## 4. Submit this form and wait for confirmation
