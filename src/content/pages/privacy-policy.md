## surrealdev.com Privacy Policy

Last updated November 9, 2026

This policy covers the surrealdev.com website, run by Surreal Development LLC ("we", "us"). The Gretio app is now operated by Gretio LLC and is covered by its own policies at [gretioapp.com](https://gretioapp.com/).

### What we collect

- **Visits.** We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits and measure page speed (pages viewed, referring site, country, browser and device type). It doesn't use cookies, doesn't track you across sites or visits, and isn't used for advertising.
- **Hosting and security.** Like any website, our hosting provider (Cloudflare) processes technical information such as your IP address to deliver pages and protect the site from abuse, and may set strictly necessary cookies to do so.
- **Contact form.** If you use the contact form, what you send is emailed to us through Microsoft 365, which also delivers your confirmation email and stores our mail, and is used only to reply to you. Cloudflare Turnstile checks that submissions aren't from bots. To limit confirmation emails, we keep a one-way hash of your email address for 24 hours.

There are no accounts, no advertising, and we don't sell personal data.

### Other websites

Embedded content (such as YouTube videos) and links to other websites are covered by those websites' own privacy policies.

### Your requests

To ask about, correct or delete anything you've sent us, use the [contact page](/contact/).

### Children

This website isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

### Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest revision.
