## Surreal Development General Privacy Policy

Last updated October 26, 2026

### 1. Introduction

Thanks for using Surreal Development's products. We need to clear a few things up about the information we collect while you are using our services. These services consist of Surreal Development LLC's websites, software applications such as the Gretio mobile app, and web services. "You" is considered the end user of the application. "Surreal Development," "we," and "us" mean Surreal Development LLC or its successor.

### 2. This Website (surrealdev.com)

The website doesn't use accounts or advertising. It uses privacy-friendly analytics to count visits and measure page speed (such as the pages viewed, the referring website, and your country, browser, and device type). These analytics don't use cookies, don't track you across websites or visits, and aren't used for advertising. Like any website, our hosting and security providers process basic technical information such as your IP address to deliver pages and protect the site from abuse, and may use strictly necessary cookies to do so.

If you submit a form, what you send is delivered to Surreal Development by email and used only to respond to you or handle your request. We use a third-party service to check that submissions aren't from bots. Embedded content (such as videos) and links to other websites are covered by those websites' own privacy policies.

### 3. What We Collect

**Account Information.** If required, the service may need to store account information or use an external service in which account information is required. This information can take the form of an email address, password, API key or token, and general meta information such as preferences. If required, this information may include personal information such as names, addresses, or billing information. Where you sign in with a provider such as Apple or Google, your credentials are handled by that provider.

**Actions and Usage Data with Online Services.** When you interact with an online service, the service may record your activities with that service. This information can consist of simple usage statistics, crash reports, IP addresses, device information, and general parameters. This data may be collected directly by the service or by using a third-party service such as Google Analytics or Firebase. This data may be related to your account information, or it may be anonymous.

**Purchase Information.** Purchases are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We receive confirmation of the purchase so that we can provide what you bought; we do not receive your payment card details.

**Location Data.** If given explicit permission, the service may record your location data such as from your mobile device. Location data may also be inferred from information such as an IP address.

**General Personal Data.** If given explicit permission, the service may record general data and statistics about you. This information can consist of vehicle information and statistics (such as a vehicle identification number and the features a vehicle supports), location data history, and other personal data which the service may be able to collect.

**Advertising Data.** Surreal Development may work with third-party advertisers. Surreal Development will not link your data with these third-party advertisers. The third-party advertisers will receive simple identifying information such as your IP address and device information. The advertiser may use this information in conjunction with other systems such as cookies to give you personalized adverts. Surreal Development does not receive any specific details about the advertisements being displayed.

### 4. Why We Collect

Surreal Development uses the data it collects for a variety of reasons.

- Maintain, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the service.
- Send you notices and newsletters.
- Monitor usage of services.
- Personalize the service and advertisements.
- Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

### 5. Reasons We Disclose Personal Data

We disclose personal data with your consent or as needed to complete a transaction or provide a service you have requested. We also share data with vendors and service providers working on our behalf, such as cloud hosting, authentication, crash reporting, and purchase validation providers, who may use it only to provide those services to us. We may disclose personal data when required by law or to respond to legal process, to protect our customers, to protect the security of our services, or to protect the rights or property of Surreal Development. We may also disclose personal data as part of a corporate transaction such as a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, in which case the successor may continue to use it under this policy. We do not sell personal data.

### 6. Withdrawal, Rectification, and Erasure

Users of our services have the right to withdraw their consents, rectify, edit, or view the data we have on record, and request the erasure of any personal data stored by Surreal Development. These rights will be suspended if Surreal Development is required to keep the information for legal purposes.

### 7. Data Storage and Information Requests

We retain personal data for as long as necessary to provide the services you use and for other legitimate purposes such as complying with legal obligations, resolving disputes, and enforcing our agreements. Vehicle records stored by Gretio are removed automatically after an extended period of inactivity. Our services are operated from the United States, and your data may be stored and processed there. We are committed to protecting the security of your personal data and use a variety of technologies and procedures to help protect it from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure, although no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Most data can be managed by you. It is your responsibility to manage your own data, including but not limited to complying with regional laws. Surreal Development is not liable for misuse or illegal use of user-uploaded data, and you assume any civil liability or criminal punishment which may arise from your uploaded content.

You may collect data on behalf of another (such as vehicle VINs). In these cases, you must collect data according to your local jurisdiction's laws. This includes responding to any formal GDPR or CCPA request where applicable. Surreal Development may forward these requests to you if they are sent to us in error.

If you have lost access to your data, use the appropriate account recovery procedures of Apple or Google before making a request to Surreal Development LLC. Surreal Development cannot manage access to third-party authentication services.

### 8. Children

Our services are not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

This policy may be changed at any time. You will be notified of this change as the revision date at the top of this document will be changed to represent the new revision.
