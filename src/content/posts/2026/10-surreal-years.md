It has officially been 10 years since I started this in college.... Almost 11 now actually...

From tinkering with some engines, to building a diagnostic powerhouse, to fighting malware, to building startups... My life's journey has been quite interesting.

I am very thankful for my thousands of supporters. I would have never gotten this far without you. And I am very grateful how many of you have stuck around over all these years.

Anyway, it is time for change.

I, Snipesy, am stepping down from the development of Project Gretio. My replacement, Eva Valentine (aka Stef), will be leading Gretio from now on. She has a keen eye for mobile dev, and is a proud member of the EFF. Gretio is in good hands. Gretio's new home going forward is at [https://gretioapp.com/](https://gretioapp.com/) under its newly founded company, Gretio LLC.

And don't worry, I'll still be around... Plotting my own adventures.
