At this time, OBDLINK v5.12.2 is not functional with Gretio. ~We are working with OBD Solutions to fix this problem.~

The issue is resolved in 5.12.4, please update your OBDLINK to 5.12.4 or newer.

~If you have not upgraded, please stay on v5.10.3.~

~If you have already upgraded, you can roll back to v5.10.3 by selecting the older firmware version in the OBDLINK App on iOS or Android.~

Play Store: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=OCTech.Mobile.Applications.OBDLink&hl=en\_US

App Store: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/obdlink/id879636351

0\. Connect to the OBDLINK with the OBDLINK App.  
1\. Tap Settings.  
2\. Tap Firmware Updates  
3\. Tap your OBDLINK.  
4\. Tap the Yellow Highlighted Version I.e. "5.12.2 (latest)”)  
5\. Tap "5.12.4”  
6\. Tap "Update Firmware”  
7\. Wait.  
8\. Disconnect OBD Link App, use Gretio as normal.

We apologize for the inconvenience.
